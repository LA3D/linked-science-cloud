Status: **proposed**. Validation: **candidate-not-generalized**. Citation integrity is not scientific truth or authorization.

## Observation

Count distinct identifiers at comparable industry granularity, disclose overlapping classifications, and retain sample material, date, units and spatial relation when enriching measurements. A nearby mapped facility is not an attributed contamination source.
