# Preserve the scientific subject and explain saved investigations without rerunning

Status: **proposed**. Validation: **candidate-not-generalized**. Citation integrity is not scientific truth or authorization.

## Observation

Keep the named disease/protein and question stable when choosing an example or recovering a failed route; label any change of biological subject and correct it when it no longer answers the task. After a successful bounded investigation, save a self-contained explanation of the question, identifier path, exact query/result, evidence settings, failed attempts, uncertainty and meaningful next question. Separate preparation probes, actual SERVICE federation, local RDF queries, ontology traversal and explicit rule-derived selection. Report execution time separately from total preparation. Provide one captured fallback and optional replay with unmistakable live-versus-saved status. In a fresh voice/presentation chat asked to explain prior work, use the saved briefing without new scientific queries; do not claim historical handles are resident or that a replay occurred. The Fabry handoff illustrates this procedure; its earlier cholesterol demo choice was explicitly superseded.

## Applicability

Fresh voice chats, scientific presentation/demo handoffs, retained REPL work, entity-preserving route changes, saved results and optional replay.

## Competing explanations

- A coherent briefing can conceal missing links unless evidence settings and assertion/derivation boundaries remain explicit.
- One observed fast query is not a guarantee of live-demo reliability.

## Missing evidence

- No independent audience comprehension or transfer evaluation.
- Live bindings may expire; saved files preserve evidence, not the original kernel.
- Detailed task directions were received through delegation; selected reports are not relabeled as original human messages.
